# Business Analysis — Stonerich Granite Construction and Supply

## Company Overview

Stonerich Granite Construction and Supply is a stone and construction supply company based in Rodriguez (Montalban), Rizal, Philippines. The company specializes in the supply and fabrication of natural and engineered stone products for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

## Business Model

- **B2B:** Wholesale supply to contractors, engineers, architects, and interior designers
- **B2C:** Retail supply and fabrication services to homeowners
- **Services:** Stone supply, fabrication, installation, and general construction supply

## Products Offered

| Category | Type |
|---|---|
| Natural Stone | Granite, Marble, Slate, Natural Stone |
| Engineered Stone | Quartz, Solid Surface, Sintered Stone |
| Landscape | Pebbles |
| Construction | Concrete Pavers |
