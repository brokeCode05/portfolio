# Competitor Analysis — Design Patterns Only

## Analysis Approach

This document analyzes design patterns from premium architecture firms, luxury stone suppliers, and modern construction companies. No content or business information is copied.

## Common Premium Design Patterns

### 1. Hero Section
- Full-screen or near-full-screen hero with high-resolution stone texture/product imagery
- Minimal overlay text with prominent CTA
- Subtle parallax or static elegance (no excessive movement)
- Dark overlay on images with light text for readability

### 2. Navigation
- Sticky, transparent-to-solid on scroll
- Minimal menu items (7-8 max)
- Mega menu or simple dropdown for products
- Prominent "Request Quote" or "Contact" CTA in nav
- Phone number visible in header

### 3. Color Usage
- Dark charcoal backgrounds for premium feel
- White/light gray for content areas
- Muted red/burgundy as accent (not dominant)
- Stone/beige tones to complement material photography
- High contrast for readability

### 4. Typography
- Serif or elegant sans-serif for headings (premium feel)
- Clean sans-serif for body text
- Large letter spacing on uppercase headings
- Generous line height for readability

### 5. Product Display
- Grid-based product categories with large imagery
- Filter/sort functionality
- Lightbox for detailed viewing
- Material texture close-ups
- Application photography showing stone in context

### 6. Trust Elements
- Clean, professional layout throughout
- High-quality photography (no stock art)
- Contact information always accessible
- Transparent business details (address, phone)
- Professional typography and spacing

## Design Patterns to Avoid

- Glassmorphism
- Floating geometric shapes
- Gradient-heavy backgrounds
- Rounded everything
- Cartoonish illustrations
- Over-animated elements
- Neon or saturated colors

## Industry Design Conventions

- **Architecture firms:** Minimal, spacious, image-forward
- **Stone suppliers:** Product-focused, texture-heavy, application imagery
- **Construction companies:** Trust-focused, service-oriented, contact-forward
